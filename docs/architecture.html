Component details

Coach · the conversation owner

Coach receives typed requests or STT transcripts. It handles intake, profile updates, nutrition tools, workout progress, and memory. It delegates plan creation to Designer and can call Retriever directly for grounded exercise answers.

A plan stays reviewable

The training schedule is part of a stored plan. Structure and Week 1 details are reviewed before approval and saving; later changes can be recorded as revisions.
